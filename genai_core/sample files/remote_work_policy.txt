REMOTE WORK POLICY Version: 1.0 Effective Date: November 26, 2026
Document Owner: Human Resources Department: Human Resources Document
Classification: Internal

1.  PURPOSE

This policy establishes guidelines for employees who are approved to
work remotely. It is intended to support flexible work arrangements
while maintaining productivity, information security, effective
communication, and reliable business operations.

2.  SCOPE

This policy applies to employees whose roles are approved for remote or
hybrid work. Remote work is not an automatic entitlement. Each
arrangement must be approved by the employee’s manager and may be
changed when business needs require.

3.  REMOTE WORK ARRANGEMENTS

Employees may be approved for one of the following arrangements:

-   Fully remote: The employee works from an approved remote location on
    all scheduled working days.
-   Hybrid: The employee divides working time between an approved remote
    location and the assigned office.
-   Occasional remote work: The employee works remotely on specific days
    with advance manager approval.

Managers determine which arrangement is appropriate based on job
responsibilities, team coverage, customer commitments, and operational
requirements.

4.  WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY

Employees must follow their normal scheduled working hours unless an
alternative schedule has been approved by their manager.

Employees working remotely must:

-   Be available during their agreed working hours.
-   Attend required meetings and respond to reasonable work-related
    communications.
-   Keep their calendar accurate and indicate planned breaks or periods
    of unavailability.
-   Notify their manager as soon as practical if an illness, power
    outage, internet failure, or other issue prevents them from working.

Remote work does not change an employee’s responsibility to meet
assigned deadlines and performance expectations.

5.  OFFICE ATTENDANCE AND TEAM MEETINGS

Employees with hybrid or occasional remote arrangements may be required
to attend the office for team meetings, training, customer meetings,
equipment support, or other business needs.

Managers should provide reasonable advance notice when an employee’s
physical attendance is required, whenever practical. Employees must
follow any office attendance schedule communicated by their manager.

6.  WORK LOCATION

Employees must work from the remote location approved by their manager.
Employees who want to change their regular remote work location must
obtain approval before making the change.

Working from another state or country may create legal, tax, payroll,
privacy, or security obligations. Employees must not work from a
different state or country without prior written approval from Human
Resources and their manager.

7.  EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES

The company may provide a laptop, monitor, keyboard, headset, or other
equipment based on role requirements and availability.

Company equipment must be used primarily for authorized business
purposes and handled with reasonable care. Employees must promptly
report lost, stolen, damaged, or malfunctioning company equipment to IT.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection
and a suitable workspace unless a different arrangement has been
approved in writing. Reimbursement of internet, phone, furniture, or
other remote-work expenses is subject to the company’s applicable
expense policy and prior approval. Employees should not assume that an
expense will be reimbursed without confirmation.

8.  INFORMATION SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees must protect company, customer, and employee information while
working remotely. They must:

-   Connect to company systems using company-approved security methods.
-   Use multi-factor authentication whenever it is required or available
    for company accounts.
-   Keep work devices protected by a password or other approved
    screen-lock method.
-   Lock their device whenever they leave it unattended.
-   Avoid discussing confidential information where unauthorized people
    may overhear.
-   Store company information only in approved company systems and
    locations.
-   Never share account credentials or allow another person to use their
    company device or account.
-   Report suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, or loss of
    company information to IT or the designated security contact
    immediately.

Employees must follow all other company information-security and
data-handling policies.

9.  HEALTH, SAFETY, AND WORKSPACE

Employees are responsible for maintaining a reasonably safe, clean, and
ergonomically suitable remote workspace. Work areas should have adequate
lighting, a stable work surface, and minimal avoidable distractions.

Employees should report work-related injuries or safety concerns through
the company’s established reporting process.

10. PERFORMANCE AND COMMUNICATION

Managers will evaluate remote employees using the same role-related
performance expectations applied to employees working at the office.
Remote employees are expected to communicate proactively, collaborate
with colleagues, and keep their managers informed about progress,
blockers, and changes that may affect deliverables.

11. CHANGES OR TERMINATION OF AN ARRANGEMENT

The company may modify, suspend, or end a remote work arrangement based
on business needs, performance concerns, security requirements, role
changes, or failure to follow this policy.

Employees who want to change their remote work schedule or arrangement
must discuss the request with their manager and obtain approval before
implementing the change.

12. QUESTIONS AND EXCEPTIONS

Questions about this policy should be directed to the employee’s manager
or Human Resources. Exceptions require prior written approval from Human
Resources and the appropriate department leader.

13. POLICY ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Employees participating in a remote work arrangement are responsible for
reading, understanding, and following this policy. This policy does not
replace applicable employment agreements, local requirements, or other
company policies.

END OF POLICY
